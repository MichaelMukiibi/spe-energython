# Challenge

## The Problem

Design a **techno-economic model** to power a **10–20 MW data center** using a **natural gas-fired
power plant** (or fuel of choice) in tropical Africa.

## Required Solution Coverage

1. **Supply chain & logistics** — fuel sourcing (CNG), truck delivery, on-site storage
2. **Power generation technologies** — plant configuration (2x1 CCGT), prime movers, waste-heat use
3. **Agreements** — PPA structure, grid interconnection (TCN 33 kV), take-or-pay fuel alignment
4. **Financial modeling** — CAPEX, OPEX, revenue, project cash flows, NPV/IRR

## Judging Weights (Finals = Shark Pitch)

| Criterion | Weight |
|---|---|
| Innovation | 20% |
| Technical rigor | 25% |
| Financial model | 25% |
| Presentation | 30% |

## Our Answer in One Line

A **2x1 combined-cycle gas turbine plant** (2x Kawasaki GPB80D + 1x Siemens SST-200 → 20.06 MWe
gross) co-located behind-the-meter to a 10–20 MW data center in Nigeria, fueled by trucked CNG,
grid-tied to the TCN 33 kV network, with surplus power exported and rated economics driven by
energy + capacity PPA revenue.

overview.
