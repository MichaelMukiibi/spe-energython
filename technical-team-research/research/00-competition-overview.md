# Competition Overview — SPE AMTS Energython 2026

## The Challenge

A student team designs a **techno-economic model** to power a **10–20 MW data center**
using a **natural gas-fired power plant** (or fuel of choice).

## Coverage Required of the Solution

1. **Supply chain & logistics** — fuel sourcing, transport, and on-site storage
2. **Power generation technologies** — plant configuration, prime movers, heat recovery
3. **Agreements** — grid interconnection, fuel supply, take-or-pay structures
4. **Financial modeling** — capital, operating costs, revenue, levelized cost

## Scoring Weights (Finals = "Shark Pitch")

| Criterion       | Weight |
| --------------- | ------ |
| Innovation      | 20%    |
| Technical rigor | 25%    |
| Financial model | 25%    |
| Presentation    | 30%    |

> Presentation is the single heaviest criterion (30%) — the finals are a live pitch.

## Team Context

- 5 members: Petroleum Eng, Mechanical Eng, Electrical Eng, 2 CS students.
- The **power plant is the core deliverable**; the data center is the demand side
  (load profiles feed the sizing and financial model).
- Competitor teams judged on the four criteria above; design must stay credible to
  manufacturers' real specs (Kawasaki GPB80D, Siemens SST-200, etc.).
