# Steam Turbine Selection — Siemens SST Series

> Merged from `So, which series do you recommend in particular un....docx` (SST series comparison).

## Recommendation

For the target application — **5–6 MW of steam power at ~500°C** — the **Siemens SST-200** is the
ideal recommendation.

## SST Series Comparison

| Feature / Spec | Siemens SST-200 | Siemens SST-400 | Siemens SST-600 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Power Range | 4 MW to 20 MW | 10 MW to 65 MW | 20 MW to 150 MW |
| Max Live Steam Temp | 540°C | 540°C | 565°C |
| Footprint / Assembly | Compact, skid-mounted single package | Intermediate casing package | Large utility-scale installation |
| Target Application | Small-scale CCGT & CHP | Medium industrial / Biomass | Large central power plants |
| CapEx Fit (for 5 MW) | **Optimal** | Over-spec / High cost | Massive over-spec |

## Core Reasons to Select the SST-200

1. **Ideal capacity match:** sized for the 4–20 MW window. An SST-400 (up to 65 MW) sized for a
   5 MW application runs at the very bottom of its efficiency curve — higher capex per MW with no benefit.
2. **Thermal capability:** handles steam up to 540°C / 120 bar. HRSG output (~500°C / 38-40 bar)
   sits comfortably within design limits, no high-alloy casing upgrades.
3. **Fast start-up & load following:** small GT CCGT plants see fluctuating exhaust temperature
   during start-up/part-load; thermo-flexible single-casing design allows rapid thermal cycling and
   faster cold/hot starts than larger frames.
4. **Skid-mounted packaged design:** pre-assembled turboset (gearbox, lube oil system, generator)
   drastically cuts civil engineering costs, piping complexity, and commissioning time.

## Recommended Specification

A **Siemens SST-200 condensing industrial steam turbine** with a dedicated water-cooled or
air-cooled surface condenser, configured for **~40–50 bar inlet pressure at 500°C**.

### Notes from verification session (`new_word` docx)

- SST-200 output range confirmed 4–20 MW; max inlet steam pressure ~110 bar, max temp ~520°C.
- Single-casing, skid-mounted, condensing or back-pressure config available.
- For a 5 MW duty the final paired generator comes from a partner OEM (Siemens' own SGen-100A line
  starts at ~25 MVA — too large); see 04-generators.md for the Marelli MJH 710 selection.
